# Phase 13: Model Governance, Privacy, and Ethical Risk Framework

## 1. Governance Principles & Model Risk Management

Responsible artificial intelligence deployment requires clear policies governing data protection, ethical fairness, reproducibility, and human accountability.

---

## 2. Privacy & Data Protection Safeguards (GDPR / CCPA)

- **Exclusion of Direct Identifiers:** Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as customer names, email addresses, social security numbers, and precise residential street addresses are strictly excluded from the model training matrix and feature schema.
- **CustomerID Hashing / Sanitization:** Account identifiers (`CustomerID`) are used exclusively as reference keys for routing predictions and are never used as model inputs.
- **Audit Log Privacy:** The telemetry monitoring logger (`src/churn_prediction/monitoring.py`) records non-PII transaction metadata (timestamp, model version, probability, risk tier, execution latency) without storing personal demographic traits or billing account numbers.
- **Right to Erasure / Rectification:** Because all preprocessing logic is deterministic and reproducible via versioned pipelines, customer records deleted from CRM databases can be safely removed from future training cohorts without corrupting model state.

---

## 3. Algorithmic Fairness & Bias Mitigation

### Demographic Feature Audit
The dataset includes demographic variables: `Gender` (`Male`/`Female`), `Senior Citizen` (`Yes`/`No`), `Partner` (`Yes`/`No`), and `Dependents` (`Yes`/`No`).

- **SHAP Sensitivity Assessment:** In Phase 6 global SHAP attribution analysis, `Gender` ranked in the bottom 2 of all features with a mean absolute SHAP impact of $< 0.005$ log-odds (effectively zero predictive weight).
- **Parity Principle:** The model's predictions are governed almost entirely by objective service characteristics (`Tenure Months`, `Contract Commitment`, `Tech Support`, `Billing Method`, and `Monthly Charges`).
- **Policy Enforcement:** Frontline retention campaigns are forbidden from offering preferential pricing based on protected demographic status; intervention rules must be grounded exclusively in subscription attributes, tenure milestones, and technical service difficulties.

---

## 4. Human-in-the-Loop Oversight

Predictions generated by this machine learning system are classified as **Decision-Support Recommendations**, not autonomous contractual actions:
- **No Involuntary Termination:** The system predicts the likelihood of customer departure; it does not trigger account cancellations, credit changes, or adverse consumer impacts.
- **Intervention Discretion:** Retention specialists and customer success managers use the risk tier badges (`High Risk`, `Medium Risk`, `Low Risk`) and local SHAP explanations as contextual guidance to tailor support calls or proactively resolve technical tickets.
- **Customer Appeal & Escalation:** In the event that a customer challenges automated billing or retention communications, standard customer service escalation paths remain active without algorithmic override.
